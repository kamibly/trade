      The paper account buys with pretend money on every new Buy signal from the scan, risking your set share of the account per trade and putting at most 20% in one symbol. It sells on the stop-loss, the target or a Sell signal. Fees of 0.1% per trade are included.

      Signals and alerts

      While the app is closed, alerts come from your server (see Your server below). With server alerts on, the app shows its own alerts on screen only, so you don't get each one twice.

      Your server

      Your trade-service on OCI sends alerts while the app is closed, scans the S&P 500 and top 50 crypto, and keeps your watchlist and holdings in sync between phones. The README in the repo's server folder shows how to set it up.

      Risk

      ATR is the average size of one candle's move, so levels adapt to how jumpy each symbol is. A new account size applies when you start the paper account over.
