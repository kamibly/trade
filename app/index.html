      About this account

      The paper account buys with pretend money on every new Buy signal from the scan, risking your set share of the account per trade and putting at most 20% in one symbol. It sells on the stop-loss, the target or a Sell signal. Each buy and sell pays the fees of the brokers picked for real trading: Interactive Brokers for stocks ($0.005 a share, at least $1) and NDAX for crypto (0.2%). Crypto paper trades buy only coins NDAX sells.

      Your server

      Your server sends alerts while the app is closed, runs the scans and the paper account, and keeps your phones in sync.

      Signals and alerts

      While the app is closed, alerts come from your server (Server alerts, under Your server). With server alerts on, the app shows its own alerts on screen only, so you don't get each one twice.

      Opportunities

      Setups your server looks for across the S&P 500 and active coins. One alerts only after it made money on average, after fees, in at least 30 past cases. The paper account trades all of them.

      Paper trading

      ATR is a candle's average move, so levels fit how jumpy each symbol is. Signals and holdings use the same stop and target. A new account size applies when you start the paper account over.

      Trailing stop: once a trade is up 1 ATR its stop moves to break even after fees, then follows the highest price at the stop-loss distance. Scans and watchlist Buys only; opportunities keep their own plan.

      Profit lock: every trade, opportunities too, keeps its target. Once it is up its full risk (2 ATR), the stop keeps half of the best gain, rising to three quarters near the target.

      It applies to new trades. Change an open one from its card. Each backtest shows all three side by side.

      Your server's machine-learning filter skips buy ideas it expects to lose and puts more into the ones it likes best. It is used only after passing its own test, and an account without it runs alongside to show whether it helps. Opportunities keep their own test.

      Real trading on NDAX

      Your server copies the paper account's crypto buys and sells to NDAX in CAD. A dry run shows the orders it would place without sending them; Live sends them.

      Once today's live losses reach the limit, no new buys until tomorrow.

      Stock trading on IBKR

      Your server copies the paper account's stock buys and sells to your IBKR paper account, in fractional shares, through IB Gateway on your VM. Paper money only for now.

      Once today's losses reach the limit, no new buys until tomorrow.
